## Social publishing, built for people and agents

We're building **Virgun** to help creators and teams plan, schedule and publish content across social platforms — and help developers bring those workflows into their own apps and AI agents.

- **For creators:** turn one idea into posts for different feeds and plan your publishing calendar.
- **For teams:** bring content and publishing workflows into one shared workspace.
- **For developers:** automate publishing through an API, SDK, CLI and MCP tools.

## Developer tools
